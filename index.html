01 / General Overview

A benchmark for current market rates.

The Oromia Construction Bureau's Market Survey and Construction Input Quality Approval Directorate implements periodic market surveys and prepares unit rates for construction works based on current market prices, gathered from producers, wholesalers, retailers and construction sites across Addis Ababa and the Oromia Sheger towns surrounding Finfinne.

Based on the data gathered, current market rates for civil works, electrical works, water supply and sanitary works, labor, and machinery & equipment were estimated — intended as a benchmark for zones across the region when estimating unit rates, contract prices, and project cost estimates.

This report is the first-quarter survey, valid for the following three months, and covers Addis Ababa, Oromia Sheger and the surrounding Finfinne area, focused on basic construction inputs. Purposive random sampling was used, with primary data obtained from questionnaire responses of construction materials manufacturers, suppliers and distributors.

03 / Survey Notes

Methodology, strengths & limitations.

Sampling & data collection: Purposive random sampling was used. Primary data was obtained from questionnaire responses of construction materials manufacturers, suppliers and distributors, and through interviews and other beneficiary input. The survey targeted factories and potential suppliers by disseminating a structured format requesting full specifications.

Limitations: The market survey covers Addis Ababa, Oromia Sheger and the surrounding Finfinne area, focused on basic construction inputs — it does not extend to other zones directly.

Problems encountered: Lack of immediate response from suppliers and distributors; lack of interest from a few suppliers to give current information; material availability varying by location within the town; some suppliers and wholesalers declined to give cost data for certain construction items.

Strengths

Good team spirit and commitment; existing well-organized preparation; patience to overcome different challenges; existing internet access and organized logistics.

Weaknesses

Survey includes only Addis Ababa and surrounding towns; lack of feedback from other core processes.
