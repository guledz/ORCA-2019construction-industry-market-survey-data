2019 Market Survey — Quantities & Price Data
Verified transcription of the Oromia Regional State Construction Authority's 2019 first-quarter market survey, covering construction input prices across Addis Ababa, Oromia Sheger, and the surrounding Finfinne area.

What's in this repo
index.html — the entire application: all 1,359 line items, search, section filters, column sorting, and CSV/JSON export. No dependencies, no build step, no external requests.
52 source sheets reproduced in original scan order, labeled Src.Img 5 through Src.Img 56.
Scope
Section	Coverage
Civil Work	Cement, rebar, aggregates, masonry, roofing, timber, glass, finishes
Electrical Work	Poles, cables, breakers, fixtures, pumps, generators
Sanitary Work	Fixtures, UPVC/PPR/HDPE pipe and fittings, tanks, valves
Labor Cost	Hourly trade rates plus monthly engineering staff rates
Machinery & Equipment	Hourly equipment rates and day rates
Features
Full-text search across item number, description, unit, and price
Section filter chips to isolate one trade at a time
Click-to-sort on any column; missing prices always sort last
CSV / JSON export of the currently filtered view
Mobile-friendly responsive layout
Data provenance
Every row was checked directly against the scanned source images rather than re-typed from an earlier draft. Errors in the prior draft — missing line items, misread digits, magnitude errors, shifted row numbering, wrong units — were corrected. Two items could not be resolved from the scan and are flagged inline as [unclear in source].

Caveats
Prices are Q1 2019 and valid for the three months following the survey.
Coverage is limited to Addis Ababa, Oromia Sheger, and surrounding Finfinne towns; it does not extend to other zones directly.
Some source rows carry no price because the original scan omitted it.
Machinery sheets 25–27 and 44 are specification text rather than priced line items; they are included for completeness.
